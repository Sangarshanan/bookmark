## The Savage Detectives

Roberto Bolaño

October 10th 2026

=========

This book was quite a journey, across characters, countries and literature. It lists more authors and poets than I can google that at one point I just started skipping sections that go on naming people. Its sometimes smart, sometimes absurd, sometimes funny but honestly never boring, it is quite non linear and I sometimes lost track of all the characters but it did not really matter even as I treated old characters as new and new ones as old because as the book goes on it becomes more about the experiences than the people.
