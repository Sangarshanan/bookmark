Midnight's children, Salman Rushdie

August 9th 2026 (Anniversary/ Breakup)

This was my first Salman Rushdie book and an introduction to colonial magical realism?! I don't really
know how to put this genre except that it has managed to leak onto me and solidify like wax on a day that
is quite important for the course of my life.

I loved the writing style, the way the world and characters were built and described, I enjoyed it being set
in India, the cities I walked around, described through the words of a Booker prize winning author, It was raw
and honest, magical and also absurd sometimes. Here are some words that stuck with me.
